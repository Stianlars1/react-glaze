# Original material studies

The owner selected the richness of Drawn To's Thermal Type, Terrain Interface and Living Terrarium references and delegated the creative direction after rejecting the first simplified sketches. The resulting collection combines a calm editorial page with three detailed physical material scenes. The original cobalt palette and generated UI tokens remain in use.

The ceramic arch and coral fibers form the default. The metallic instrument and miniature moss world offer contrasting material detail behind the same genuine glass component. They are generated artwork, not evidence of live 3D, heat, water or botanical simulation. Motion comes from direct glass manipulation, material selection and the package's pointer rim.

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Its interface does not expose an explicit model selector. The original PNG files are retained under public/art; no AI-painted UI or glass overlay substitutes for the component. The complete prompts used follow.

Saved assets: [Soft forms](../public/art/soft.png), [Hard light](../public/art/metal.png), [Small world](../public/art/garden.png).
